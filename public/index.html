      How it works
      
      
      
        
      Walk — tap the floor. Sit — tap a chair, sofa or beanbag. Pan — drag. Zoom — pinch, scroll, or the +/− buttons.
      
        
      Grab a drink — tap the kombucha fridge or the cold brew bar. The gong — ring it when a PR merges. The big red button — deploys to prod. Obviously.
      
        
      Tap anyone to see who they are and how many PRs they've merged. The wall of fame on the left wall lights up a pixel for every contributor who's visited.
