        How it works
        
      
        
        
        Getting around
        
        
          
          
        • Walk — tapclick the floor.
          • 
          
        • Sit — tapclick a chair, sofa or beanbag. Stand up — walk somewhere else, or Sit / stand in the emote tray.
          • 
          
        • Look around — drag the room. Zoom — pinch.scroll, or the + and − buttons at the edge of the room.
          • 
        
        
        
        People
        
        
          
          
        • TapClick anyone to see who they are, wave at them or whisper.
          • 
          
        • The green count at the top lists everyone inside.
          • 
          
        • The smiley next to the chat box opens emotes (wave, dance, sit, the gong) and reactions.
          • 
          
        • The shirt button changes your look.
          • 
        
        
        
        The room
        
        
          
          
        • Grab a drink — the kombucha fridge or the cold brew bar.
          • 
          
        • The gong — ring it when a PR merges. The big red button — deploys to prod. Obviously.
          • 
          
        • The wall of fame on the left wall lights up a pixel for every contributor who's visited.
          • 
        
        
        
        Chat commands
        
        
          
          
        • /wave /dance /sit /stand /afk
          • 
          
        • /me does a thing — say it in the third person
          • 
          
        • /w @someone hi — whisper (only you two see it)
          • 
          
        • /who — who's here · /stats — lounge numbers · /gong
          • 
        
        
        
        Enter or / jumps to the chat box. Esc closes panels.
        
        
        The window shows the valley in real  time. The signs on the buildings keep rebranding. That's just how it is out there.
